Behavioral strategist · Researcher · Science communicator

Jori Grym

I am a Behavioral Strategist at the Finnish Tax Administration and a Behavioral Science Researcher at Hanken School of Economics. My research focuses on moral judgment and ethical decision-making. I also work on AI adoption and how AI will change the working environment. I teach consumer and marketing psychology, regularly appear as a science communicator on Yle's Kulttuuriykkönen Perjantaistudio radio show, and was a regular panelist on Yle's Jälkikaronkka science program.
